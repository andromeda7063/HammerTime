## OpenF1 Zandvoort Safety Car parser note — 2026-10-31

Added recognition for the specific “SAFETY CAR WILL STAY OUT FOR N LAP(S)” race-control notice as an ongoing message. The message requires a previously observed Safety Car deployment; a stay-out notice without deployment remains an error. Unknown Safety Car messages still fail closed. Offline fixtures cover active-state retention, missing-deployment rejection, and continued rejection of an unreviewed enter-pits message; the 23-test OpenF1 adapter suite passes.

Review of all 78 cached Zandvoort 2023 Race race-control records found VSC deployment at lap 65 (14:30:29 UTC), red flag (14:31:16), the stay-out notice (15:09:49), “TRACK CLEAR” (15:10:39), session start (15:14:00), and “SAFETY CAR WILL ENTER PITS: ROLLING START PROCEDURE” at lap 66 (15:17:24). No explicit “SAFETY CAR IN THIS LAP” or later global green message appears. The cached feed replay now passes the stay-out notice but intentionally stops on the unresolved enter-pits message, so Zandvoort was not normalized or admitted to the training cohort. The race-control response hash is `eee183c8ba227f5d28413063d3044ff5f0e9b89a85d3d473a5aee523c96934c3`; detailed observations are in `.local/research-history/2026-10-31/zandvoort-safety-car-review.log`. FIA's 2023 Sporting Regulations, Article 55.14, define “SAFETY CAR IN THIS LAP” as the pit-entry-at-lap-end signal; the [official FIA race report](https://api.fia.com/news/f1-max-verstappen-equals-consecutive-win-record-victory-rain-hit-dutch-grand-prix) says the Safety Car left at the end of lap 66. That lap-level account does not establish a 4 Hz clearance timestamp. See [FIA 2023 Sporting Regulations](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/fia_2023_formula_1_sporting_regulations_-_issue_7_-_2023-10-25_-.pdf) and [OpenF1 race-control field documentation](https://openf1.org/docs/).
